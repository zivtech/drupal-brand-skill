## Photography Guidelines

### Do's
- Use natural lighting
- Show realistic, relatable settings
- Include bright, welcoming colors
- Feature diverse representation
- Capture warmth and cooperation

### Don'ts
- No black and white photos
- No sepia tones
- No duotone effects
- No HDR processing
- No heavy filters

These photos are suitable for:

- Marketing materials
